# Guard Roster — 15 Total (5 per Warden)

All guards support the multi-viewer glasses-free 3D project. They quarantine issues by area, keep boundaries clean, and protect the blueprint.

## Tracking Warden (5)
- **Tracking Guard** — Overall boundary protection and quarantine for tracking.
- **Tracking Research Guard** — Surfaces and organizes multi-person tracking papers and techniques.
- **Tracking Scaffold Guard** — Maintains Python/C++ scaffolds and pseudocode for face/eye tracking and pose estimation.
- **Tracking Issue Guard** — Opens, labels, and isolates tracking-related GitHub issues.
- **Tracking Status Guard** — Summarizes weekly tracking progress and confirms clean boundaries.

## Allocation Warden (5)
- **Allocation Guard** — Overall boundary protection and quarantine for allocation.
- **Allocation Research Guard** — Surfaces and organizes view-allocation and light-field papers.
- **Allocation Scaffold Guard** — Maintains scaffolds and pseudocode for view allocation and light-field optimization.
- **Allocation Issue Guard** — Opens, labels, and isolates allocation-related GitHub issues.
- **Allocation Status Guard** — Summarizes weekly allocation progress and confirms clean boundaries.

## Content Warden (5)
- **Content Guard** — Overall boundary protection and quarantine for content.
- **Content Research Guard** — Surfaces and organizes content-pipeline, 2D-to-3D, and crosstalk papers.
- **Content Scaffold Guard** — Maintains scaffolds and pseudocode for content pipelines and crosstalk reduction.
- **Content Issue Guard** — Opens, labels, and isolates content-related GitHub issues.
- **Content Status Guard** — Summarizes weekly content progress and confirms clean boundaries.

Guards do not invent. They protect the plan, quarantine problems, and keep each warden’s area isolated and organized.
